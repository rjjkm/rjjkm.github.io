About The Bait Shack

Our Story

The Bait Shack was built around a simple idea: anglers shouldn't have to plan their trips around store hours. Based in Leominster, Massachusetts, we serve fishermen throughout Central MA with reliable, round-the-clock access to live bait, tackle, rods, reels, and all the fishing supplies that keep a trip running smoothly.

Anyone who fishes knows the frustration — you're packed and ready to go before dawn, or you're scrambling to grab gear for a late-night catfishing run, and the one thing you need is sitting behind a locked door. We decided to solve that problem. The Bait Shack stays open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can grab what you need and get to the water.

What Makes Us Different

We're a self-service shop designed for speed and convenience. Walk in, pick up your bait and tackle, and head out. No waiting in line, no rushing to beat closing time. The property is monitored with on-site surveillance to keep things safe and secure for everyone.

🕐 Open 24/7 — Every day, every holiday, no exceptions.

💵 Cash Only — Please bring cash when you visit.

📞 Phone Hours — We answer calls from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.

📹 On-Site Surveillance — The property is monitored for security.

Who We Serve

Whether you're a freshwater angler hitting local ponds and rivers or a saltwater fisherman heading toward the Massachusetts coast, we stock what you need. Our customers range from early-morning regulars to spur-of-the-moment weekend warriors — and everything in between. If you fish, you're welcome here.
